Offers a more limited feature set compared to the professional edition.
- Proxy
	- Interception and modification of request and responses while interacting with web application.
- Repeater
	- Allows for capturing, modifying, and resending the same request multiples times.
- Intruder
	- Despite rate limitations in Burp Suite Community, allows for spraying endpoints with requests.
	- It's commonly utilized for brute-force attacks or fuzzing endpoints.
- Decoder
	- Offer a valuable service for data transformation. It can decode captured information or encode payloads before sending them to the target.
- Comparer
	- Enable the comparison of two pieces of data at either the word or byte level.
- Sequencer
	- Typically employed when assessing the randomness token, such as session cookie values or other supposedly randomly generated data.
