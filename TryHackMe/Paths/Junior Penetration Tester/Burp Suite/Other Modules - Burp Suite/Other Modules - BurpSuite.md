## Sequencer
Sequencer allows us to evaluate the entropy, or randomness, of "tokens".

### Sequencer - Analysis
- Overall result
	- This gives a broad assessment of the token generation mechanism.
- Effective entropy
	- This measures the randomness of the tokens.
- Reliability
	- The significance level of 1% implies that there is 99% confidence in the accuracy of results.
- Sample
	- This provides details about the token samples analyzed during the entropy testing process.
